# PSX Liminal Kit Free

**15 free modular PS1-style pieces to build the backrooms** for your horror game: yellow carpet dry and damp, the ceiling tiles plain, with a fluorescent light that hums and with a tile missing, the yellow wallpaper clean and stained, a doorway, a wide opening, a corner post and a pillar, the yellow door, the door that stands on its own in the middle of a room, a green EXIT sign and a wall vent. They snap to a 2 m grid. Low-poly and point-filtered, a scene per piece with its colliders, ready to drop in. Free for commercial use.

### Want more?

**[PSX Liminal Kit](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-liminal-kit)** has 142 pieces for the
backrooms, the poolrooms and an empty playplace: a pool sunk in with its steps, ladders, slide
and water, white tile and arches, a ball pit, a play tower and a claw machine, a hotel's hall with
its room doors, sconces and a lift, a hotel room, working machines, a Unity package and a level
to walk and solve in your browser. Same addon, same look: it installs over this one.

For more of the same grid, [PSX Mall Kit Free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-mall-kit-free)
and [PSX Gas Station Kit Free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-gas-station-kit-free) have 15

### What's inside

- **Structure:** yellow carpet dry and damp, the ceiling tiles plain, lit and with a tile
  missing, the yellow wall clean and stained, a doorway, a 1.6 m opening, a corner post and a
  pillar, on a 2 m grid, 3 m high.
- **Doors and fittings:** the yellow door, the door to nowhere, both swinging open, their
  colliders moving with them; a green EXIT sign and a wall vent.
- **2 animations** on the moving parts, as clips in GLB and takes in FBX.
- **15 icons**, 256x256 PNGs with transparency, and the seamless floor, wall and ceiling
  textures as their own PNGs.
- One 1024x1024 texture atlas, shading baked into the vertex colours, real-world scale.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with a scene per piece: box colliders on every part (a doorway's leave the way through
clear), an AnimationPlayer where it moves, one lit material so your lights fall on it, and a
gallery. Tested on Godot 4.3 and 4.7, on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

```gdscript
# door: the yellow door in your level
door.get_node("AnimationPlayer").play("open")
```

For the full PS1 look, [PSX Look](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look) turns the kit (and your
level) over in one line: wobbling vertices, warping textures, 240p, dither and 15-bit colour. Its
[free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look-free) has the vertex snap and affine textures.
